Exploratory Data Analysis & Visualizations

Statistical distributions and correlation patterns discovered during exploratory investigation of the loan dataset.

1. Overall Loan Approval Distribution
Baseline portfolio exhibits 68.7% approval rate (422 applicants) versus 31.3% rejection rate (192 applicants), demonstrating moderate class imbalance.
2. Credit History Decisive Impact
Applicants with Good Credit (1.0) achieve >79% approval, while those with Poor/Default History (0.0) suffer >92% rejection.
3. Geographic Property Sector
Semiurban properties demonstrate the highest institutional approval rate (~76.8%), followed by Urban (~65.8%) and Rural (~61.5%).
4. Education Level Correlation
University graduates maintain higher baseline approval rates due to superior employment stability and higher lifetime earning trajectories.
5. Income Skewness & Outliers
Applicant income exhibits strong right-skewness with extreme high-net-worth outliers. Feature engineering transforms this into Total Income & Balance Disposable Income.
6. Correlation Heatmap
Numerical correlation matrix highlights strong positive association between Loan Amount and Applicant Income, as well as Credit History and approval status.
7. Multi-Metric Machine Learning Algorithm Comparison
Side-by-side performance of Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting across Accuracy, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC metrics.
