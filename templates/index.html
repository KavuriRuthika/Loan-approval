Enterprise ML Underwriting Pipeline

From raw applicant demographics to risk-weighted credit determinations in under 50 milliseconds.

1. Applicant Intake & Validation

Captures income, co-applicant financial contributions, requested principal, term duration, and property demographic classification.

2. Financial Stress Metrics

Computes Debt-to-Income (DTI), Equated Monthly Installment (EMI), Total Household Liquidity, and disposable residual income.

3. Multi-Model Inference

Passes normalized engineered features through our trained Random Forest ensemble to generate probability distributions.

4. Explainability & Risk Rating

Outputs transparent confidence percentages, assigns Risk Tiers (Low, Moderate, High), and details primary driving underwriting factors.
